## 🔑 5-POINT ANSWERS (Quick Recall)

### 5 Benefits of Startup India:
1. 3-year income tax holiday
2. 80% rebate on patent filing
3. Self-certification under labour laws
4. Fund of Funds (₹10,000 Cr)
5. Fast winding up (90 days under IBC)

### 5 MUDRA Facts:
1. Launched: 2015
2. Shishu: Up to ₹50,000
3. Kishor: ₹50,000 – ₹5 lakh
4. Tarun: ₹5 lakh – ₹10 lakh
5. No collateral required

### 5 Components of an Executive Summary:
1. Problem being solved
2. Solution / product description
3. Target market (TAM)
4. Business model (how you earn)
5. Funding ask (how much needed)

### 5 Responsibilities of IT Professionals (ACM):
1. Contribute to society
2. Avoid harm
3. Be honest and trustworthy
4. Be fair and non-discriminatory
5. Respect privacy

### 5 Patent Requirements in India:
1. Novel (new)
2. Non-obvious
3. Useful (industrial application)
4. Disclosed in application
5. Not excluded under Section 3
