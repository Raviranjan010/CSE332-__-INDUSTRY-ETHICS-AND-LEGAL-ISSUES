*Answer: TRUE*

*Answer: TRUE*

*Answer: FALSE (Bootstrapping means self-funding from personal savings)*

*Answer: TRUE*

**Q92.** Zerodha took venture capital funding to become profitable.
*Answer: FALSE (Zerodha is entirely bootstrapped)*

*Answer: TRUE*

*Answer: FALSE (They differ in eligibility, size, sector coverage, and benefits)*
