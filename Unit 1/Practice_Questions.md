## 📌 PART D: 10 SHORT ANSWER QUESTIONS (2-3 Marks)

**Q86. Define ethics and state its primary purpose.**
* *Answer:* Ethics is the branch of philosophy that studies human conduct to determine what is right or wrong, good or bad. Its primary purpose is to establish standard moral guidelines that promote trust, fairness, and responsibility in human society.

**Q87. Explain the concept of integrity with a simple example.**
* *Answer:* Integrity is the quality of consistently following moral principles, even when unsupervised or under pressure. Example: A student refusing to look at notes during an online, unproctored exam, choosing to write only what they know.

**Q88. How is ethics different from law?**
* *Answer:* Law is a set of rules enforced by the state with legal sanctions (fines, jail). Ethics is a set of moral guidelines followed voluntarily, carrying internal (guilt) or social (reputation loss) sanctions.

**Q89. What is Mason's PAPA framework in IT ethics?**
* *Answer:* The PAPA framework outlines the four core ethical issues of the digital age:
  1. **Privacy:** Data collection limits.
  2. **Accuracy:** Correctness of stored data.
  3. **Property:** Ownership of digital code/media.
  4. **Accessibility:** Right to access information.

**Q90. What is software piracy? Mention its types.**
* *Answer:* Software piracy is using or copying software without a valid license. Types include:
  1. End-user piracy (extra installations).
  2. Internet piracy (illegal downloads).
  3. Hard-disk loading (pre-installing pirated software on sold machines).
  4. Counterfeiting (selling duplicates).

**Q91. Why is the "Tone at the Top" critical for corporate ethics?**
* *Answer:* If executive leaders model ethical behavior, employees follow their example. If leaders compromise on honesty or safety, it signals to employees that unethical behavior is acceptable.

**Q92. Explain the difference between Utilitarianism and Deontology.**
* *Answer:* Utilitarianism is consequentialist; it chooses the action that yields the greatest good for the majority. Deontology is rule-based; it demands compliance with moral duties absolutely, regardless of outcomes.

**Q93. What are the key components of a corporate Code of Conduct?**
* *Answer:*
  1. Core values and mission statement.
  2. Guidelines on employee conduct and professionalism.
  3. Conflicts of interest and anti-bribery policies.
  4. Safe reporting channels (whistleblower hotlines).

**Q94. Name two Indian government initiatives supporting women empowerment in technology.**
* *Answer:*
  1. **Digital Sakhi:** Providing digital financial literacy to rural women.
  2. **Beti Bachao Beti Padhao:** Promoting girl child education and welfare.

**Q95. Explain the ethical issue of biopiracy with an example.**
* *Answer:* Biopiracy is patenting traditional indigenous knowledge by foreign corporations without consent or compensation. Example: US companies trying to patent the pesticide properties of Neem trees, which India successfully overturned.

## 📌 PART E: 5 LONG / CASE STUDY QUESTIONS (10 Marks)

**Q96. Discuss the Bhopal Gas Tragedy (1984) as a catastrophic failure of engineering ethics.**
* *Answer:*
  * **The Event:** Over 40 tons of toxic methyl isocyanate (MIC) gas leaked from the Union Carbide plant in Bhopal, killing over 15,000 people and injuring 500,000+.
  * **Ethical Failures:**
    1. *Prioritizing cost cuts over safety:* Safety scrubbers and cooling systems were turned off or poorly maintained to save money.
    2. *Ignoring early warnings:* Minor leaks had occurred earlier, but management ignored warnings from workers.
    3. *Lack of emergency plan:* No warning system or evacuation drill was active for the surrounding community.
  * **Conclusion:** Engineers and managers failed their primary ethical duty to prioritize public safety.

**Q97. Explain the PAPA framework and discuss how it applies to social media companies like Facebook or TikTok.**
* *Answer:*
  * **Privacy:** Platforms harvest massive user profiles, location histories, and likes. Ethical issues arise when data is sold to advertisers without explicit, granular user consent.
  * **Accuracy:** Misinformation, fake news, and deepfakes spread rapidly. Platforms face ethical pressure to filter content and ensure data veracity.
  * **Property:** Who owns the uploaded content? While users hold copyrights, platforms terms often grant them global, royalty-free licenses to use user media.
  * **Accessibility:** Algorithms can exclude certain populations or show bias. Ensuring accessibility for disabled users and equal distribution of access is a key ethical requirement.

**Q98. Differentiate between Honesty, Integrity, and Reputation. Explain how they connect.**
* *Answer:*
  * **Honesty:** Telling the truth in communications (words).
  * **Integrity:** Living by moral principles consistently in both words and actions, especially when unobserved (character).
  * **Reputation:** What the public thinks of you (external image).
  * **Connection:**
    * Honesty is a vital component of integrity.
    * Integrity is your true internal character, while reputation is your external image.
    * A person can have a good reputation but poor integrity (secretly cheating). Over the long term, lack of integrity eventually destroys reputation.

**Q99. Case Study: Buggy Software Release**
*Situation: A lead software engineer discovers a major memory leak in a flight booking system 2 days before launch. Fixing it will delay release by 2 weeks, costing the company ₹50 lakh. The manager orders the engineer to ship it and patch it later.*
* **Analysis:**
  1. *Identify Stakeholders:* Users (flyers), airline clients, corporate employer, development team.
  2. *Apply Ethical Frameworks:*
     * *Deontology:* Shipping known defective code violates the rule of delivering quality work (unethical).
     * *Utilitarianism:* A system crash mid-flight booking could disrupt travel plans for thousands, causing greater collective harm than the ₹50 lakh loss.
  3. *Action:* The engineer should refuse to sign off on the build, document the technical risks, and present them to senior leadership to secure a delayed, safe launch.

    2. *Meesho:* Empowers homemakers to run digital retail businesses using smartphones, securing financial independence.
