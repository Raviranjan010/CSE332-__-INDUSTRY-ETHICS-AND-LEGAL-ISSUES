**Q1. Ethics is best defined as the branch of knowledge that studies:**
- A) Quantitative statistical trends in human population over time.
- B) **Standards of right and wrong conduct governing human behavior.** ✅
- C) Financial accounting guidelines in corporate structures.
- D) Legal statutes adopted by national assemblies.
- E) Subjective personal preferences without logical backing.
- F) Mathematical algorithms for database optimization.

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Ethics is the systematic study of moral principles that guide human actions, helping us decide what is right and wrong. Demography (A), accounting (C), positive law (D), personal preference (E), and computer science (F) are unrelated fields.

**Q2. Integrity is distinct from honesty because integrity requires:**
- A) Merely stating the truth under court oath.
- B) **Consistency between values and actions, even when unsupervised.** ✅
- C) Adhering to laws only when being monitored by cameras.
- D) Being loyal to your company regardless of their illegal acts.
- E) Keeping secrets even if they harm public safety.
- F) Maximizing short-term profit at any personal cost.

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Integrity requires wholeness of character and consistent adherence to moral principles, particularly when unobserved (doing the right thing when nobody is watching). Simple honesty (A) is telling the truth, but integrity is practicing it consistently.

**Q3. Richard Mason's PAPA framework in IT ethics stands for:**

- D) Privacy, Accuracy, Property, Accountability

**Explanation:** The PAPA framework (Mason, 1986) outlines the four core ethical areas in the information age: Privacy (data collection), Accuracy (data correctness), Property (data ownership), and Accessibility (data access rights).
