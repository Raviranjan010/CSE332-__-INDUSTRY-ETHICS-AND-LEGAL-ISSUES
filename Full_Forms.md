### 📌 Section 2: Additional Syllabus Acronyms (IT Ethics, Startup planning & Cybersecurity)

| Acronym | Full Form / Meaning | Context / Focus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **PAPA** | Privacy, Accuracy, Property, Accessibility | Richard Mason's foundational IT Ethics framework. |
| **ACM** | Association for Computing Machinery | Standard code of ethics for software professionals. |
| **IEEE** | Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers | Engineering code of ethics prioritizing public safety. |
| **DPIIT** | Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade | The current body managing Startup India recognitions. |
| **TAM** | Total Addressable Market | Market sizing: the global demand for a startup's category. |
| **SAM** | Serviceable Addressable Market | Market sizing: the geographic or operational portion of TAM. |
| **SOM** | Serviceable Obtainable Market | Market sizing: the realistic portion of SAM captured in the short term. |
| **MVP** | Minimum Viable Product | Early product version for quick user validation with low cost. |
| **ESOP** | Employee Stock Option Plan | Equity options issued to employees for retention. |
| **CAC** | Customer Acquisition Cost | Total expense required to acquire a new customer. |
| **LTV** | Lifetime Value | Net revenue expected from a single customer account over time. |
| **MRR / ARR** | Monthly Recurring Revenue / Annual Recurring Revenue | Key financial metrics for subscription-based SaaS startups. |
| **KPI** | Key Performance Indicator | Operations metric to monitor business growth. |
| **CI / CD** | Continuous Integration / Continuous Deployment | Modern automated software build and release pipeline. |
| **FDI** | Foreign Direct Investment | Equity capital injection by a foreign company into domestic entities. |
| **ITES** | IT-Enabled Services | Business processes executed remotely using IT infrastructure. |
| **BPO** | Business Process Outsourcing | Contracting non-primary business activities to service providers. |
| **KPO** | Knowledge Process Outsourcing | Advanced information processing (e.g. business analytics, market research). |
| **LPO** | Legal Process Outsourcing | Outsourced legal services (e.g., patent filing, contract review). |
| **WIPO** | World Intellectual Property Organization | UN agency governing global IP policies. |
| **BIS** | Bureau of Indian Standards | National standards body in India overseeing certifications (ISI, etc.). |
| **ISI** | Indian Standards Institution | Mark certifying safety/quality standards on devices and appliances. |
| **IPC** | Indian Penal Code | Penal code in India (e.g., Section 420 used in online cheating). |
| **LLP** | Limited Liability Partnership | Partnership structure providing limited liability to owners. |
| **IBC** | Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code | Law governing winding up (fast-track exit in 90 days for startups). |
| **AUP** | Acceptable Use Policy | Policy document outlining permitted/prohibited network activities. |
| **SIEM** | Security Information and Event Management | Log aggregation and security monitoring system. |
| **EDR** | Endpoint Detection and Response | Endpoint cybersecurity threat detection system. |
| **IDS / IPS** | Intrusion Detection / Prevention System | System for detecting or blocking network intrusion. |
| **VPN** | Virtual Private Network | Encrypted communication tunnel over public networks. |
| **SPF / DKIM** | Sender Policy Framework / DomainKeys Identified Mail | Email authentication protocols to prevent spoofing. |
| **DMARC** | Domain-based Message Authentication, Reporting... | Policy protocol checking SPF/DKIM verification. |
| **SBIR** | Small Business Innovation Research | US R&D grant program for commercializing innovations. |
| **STTR** | Small Business Technology Transfer | US R&D grant program requiring university research collaboration. |
| **NSF** | National Science Foundation | US agency funding scientific innovation through research grants. |
| **CGTMSE** | Credit Guarantee Fund Trust for Micro & Small Enterprises | Collateral-free loans guarantee trust up to ₹2 crore in India. |
